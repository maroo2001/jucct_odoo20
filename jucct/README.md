# 🎓 Educational Center Management System

## 📘 Project Overview
The **Educational Center Management System** is an **Odoo-based platform** designed to help an educational center manage collaborations with multiple academies.

Each academy offers **courses** and **diplomas**, each with defined **prices**, **term** and **schedules**.  
The system simplifies handling **contracts**, **student registrations**, **payments**, and **inquiries**.

---

## 🧭 Scope

### 🏫 Academy Management
Manage partner academies and their related courses or diplomas.

### 📚 Course & Diploma Management
Add and organize all training programs with details such as **duration**, **price**, and **availability**.

### 🧍 Student Registration
Register students in available and active courses or diplomas based on their **preferences** and **schedule**.

### 💳 Payment Tracking
Manage tuition payments with up to **10 installments per student**, tracking **paid** and **remaining balances**.

### 📄 Contracts Management
Record and monitor contracts between the center and each academy, including **profit-sharing** details.

### 💬 Inquiries Management
Record and follow up on **student inquiries or requests** to enhance service and communication.
